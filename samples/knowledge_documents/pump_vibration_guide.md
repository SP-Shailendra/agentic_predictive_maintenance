## Centrifugal pump vibration review

Document type: Troubleshooting guide  
Manufacturer: Example Pumps  
Asset type: Centrifugal pump  
Revision: Sample-01

### Observation

An elevated vibration reading is a symptom, not proof that a bearing has failed. Confirm the asset tag, sensor identity, timestamp, operating state, and the configured advisory limit before comparing readings.

### Suggested review topics

- Compare vibration readings across the available time window and check whether the sensor source is synthetic or external.
- Review coupling alignment, mounting looseness, lubrication history, and recent maintenance records using the applicable site procedure.
- If the applicable procedure permits, collect the required measurements using calibrated equipment and qualified personnel.
